What is Father's Love?

FATHER'S LOVE is the response of children to the love of their Father. It is a call to draw near to God, to behold His heart, and to respond with surrendered lives and wholehearted worship.

Rooted in the revelation of God's love through Jesus Christ, FATHER'S LOVE exists to cultivate spaces where believers can encounter His presence, grow in intimacy with Him, and live in joyful obedience to His voice.

The implication of encountering the Father's love is transformation. As children who have received His love, our natural response is surrender, worship, and devotion. We bow our hearts before Him, acknowledging His lordship and supremacy over every area of our lives.

Thus, we proclaim the truth of Romans 14:11: "As I live, says the Lord, every knee shall bow to Me, and every tongue shall confess to God." Every Knee Bow is one expression of that response—a gathering of God's children united in worship, reverence, and love for their Father.

About Convenor

Meshakh Mulliro, is a devotee of Jesus Christ, serving as a Christian Exegete, Servant-Worship Leader, and a teacher of the Word in formation. His life is anchored in a deep devotion to Christ and a commitment to rightly dividing the Word of God with reverence, clarity, and spiritual depth. He is the Convenor of Every Knee Bow, formerly referred to as Worship Resonance, a worship expression rooted in Romans 14:11 that exists to gather worshippers into sincere, undistracted encounter with God centered on the supremacy of Christ.

Meshakh's interests lie in worship development, music excellence, and spiritual formation within worship teams. He has pursued formal training at Music-Inn International School of Music and RedFouth Music Academy, strengthening his foundation in music and worship leadership. He is particularly focused on integrating musical skill, spiritual sensitivity, and structured team dynamics to cultivate effective worship environments.
Meshakh's ministry conviction is centered on creating sacred spaces where worship, the Word, and life converge. He serves as a Worship Development Coach, working with worship teams on spiritual formation, vocal development, stage coordination, and team alignment.

Beyond ministry, Meshakh operates in the B2B growth and digital systems space, specializing in CRM Consultancy, lead generation, SEO, and web development. He works with businesses to design and implement structured systems that improve customer acquisition, conversion, and retention. His professional focus is on building scalable digital ecosystems that integrate automation, marketing strategy, and customer experience optimization.

Meshakh's vision for Every Knee Bow is to see it become a recurring, transformative worship experience that continually draws worshippers into deeper encounter with God. He envisions it as a catalyst for spiritual awakening, revival, and the strengthening of the church's worship culture in Nairobi and beyond.

He is also the Founder and Lead of SkillForward Youth, an edutech NGO focused on youth development. Through this initiative, he leads a structured programme that integrates digital awareness—understanding how digital life shapes thinking, attention, and behaviour—alongside digital literacy, digital fluency, AI capability, and practical life skills. The programme is designed to equip young Kenyans aged 13–25 to participate meaningfully in the digital economy, blending interpersonal effectiveness with modern technological competence and applied AI skills.

Father's Love is his annual or bi-annual gathering.
